The World Bank’s 5 November report provides the latest developing-country debt-scale baseline. These reinforce fiscal and refinancing pressure, but no distinct sovereign distress or broad repricing event in the 16–23 November window warrants a score change, so Debt Control 89 and Rebel Resistance 26 are held.

    Fragmented Governance Weak Spots

    Since the prior run, Reuters reported Ethiopian and Eritrean forces massing near their border. ACLED’s profile says EDF forces retook Badme and moved toward Tigray; it also reports a 19 November drone strike on Badme with attribution unspecified, while its 20 November update noted troop movements near the border but no reported active fighting. These are escalation risks, not confirmation of renewed war. Reuters also reported Bangladesh protest violence amid a politically charged return standoff. Together the fragmentation signals support a cautious one-point paired move: Governance Control 78 → 79 and Rebel Resistance 68 → 69.
