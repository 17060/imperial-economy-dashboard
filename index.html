    Energy Chokepoints

    IEA’s 13 November Oil Market Report says Gulf oil exports reached 18.4 mb/d in October, still 26% below pre-war levels; refined-product and LPG exports remain about half of pre-war levels. Reuters reports LNG crossings increased in October while ship attacks also rose, with at least 10 vessels hit in November’s first 10 days. The 15 November territorial shift near Bab el-Mandeb adds route risk, but does not establish a closure. Energy scores remain Imperial Control 100 and Rebel Resistance 12.

    Debt Leverage

    Credit is obedience written in future tense. The World Bank’s 5 November International Debt Report, published before the prior run, puts developing-country external debt at $9.4 trillion in 2025 and debt-service payments at $1.4 trillion. IMF’s October analysis reports G7 10-year yields rose more than one percentage point since February to a near-20-year high, and estimates global public debt may exceed 100% of GDP by 2030. No comparable post-9-November yield or distress step-change was verified, so Debt Control 89 and Rebel Resistance 26 are held.

    Fragmented Governance Weak Spots

    Freedom House's Freedom in the World 2026 records a 20th consecutive year of global freedom decline through 2025. Since the prior run, Reuters reported Yemeni government forces withdrew from an area overlooking Bab el-Mandeb on 15 November, leaving the Houthis in control; the report does not say the strait is closed. ACLED’s 13 November update covers further regional fractures and separates claims from independently verified incidents. Governance Control rises 76 → 78 and Rebel Resistance 66 → 68, reflecting increased leverage in contested territory alongside fragmented authority.

    AI Surveillance Readiness

    Stanford HAI’s 11 November public-administration brief reports 3,611 AI use cases across 56 federal agencies in 2025, up 69%; it indicates adoption breadth, not a specific new surveillance deployment. Its 2026 AI Index documents concentrated model and compute supply alongside extensive open-source activity. Freedom House’s 12 November brief describes satellite connectivity as a potential response to shutdowns and Iran’s efforts to restrict it.     Crypto Settlement & Regulatory Perimeter

    Dual-use axis. FinCEN’s MEXC order was published in the Federal Register on 10 November and takes effect 10 December 2026, prohibiting covered financial institutions from transmittals of funds to or from MEXC Global. On 13 November, the CFTC sued Louisiana and Arkansas over state actions targeting federally regulated event-contract venues; these are unresolved jurisdiction disputes, not final rulings or new crypto enforcement actions. FinCEN’s transaction statements are agency findings, not court-adjudicated findings.
