    Imperial Control Rebel Resilience

    Governance is the only axis where Rebel resilience is within striking distance of Imperial Control — polycentric institutions remain the structural defense the Empire cannot fully encircle. Energy and AI/digital are the cycle's pressure points; crypto and debt sit in a holding pattern.

    Crypto Rails as Rebel Finance

    Crypto remains dual-use in the control loop. The Imperial perimeter tightened through August 2026: OFAC’s 7 August action targeted Iran-linked exchanges; the EU’s 21st Russia-sanctions package bans transactions with 14 crypto platforms and creates a first-use full third-country crypto-service ban; the SEC’s 18 August Regulation Crypto Assets proposal adds a supervised offering regime. MiCA wind-down and stablecoin sanctions programs remain active. Self-hosted wallets remain an explicit alternative, but regulated gateways are narrower. The high-level mechanisms below describe — analytically, non-operationally — where decentralized finance still preserves settlement and custody optionality.

    Polycentric Governance as Structural Defense

    Freedom House's Freedom in the World 2026 records a 20th consecutive year of global freedom decline — 54 countries declined vs 35 improved. But the same report still tallies 88 Free polities and the Imperial axis fails to consolidate where federalism, independent judiciaries, free press, and legal pluralism overlap.
