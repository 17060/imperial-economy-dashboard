    Crypto Rails as Rebel Finance

    Crypto remains dual-use in the control loop. FinCEN’s MEXC special measure was published in the Federal Register on 10 November and takes effect 10 December 2026, prohibiting covered financial institutions from transmittals to or from MEXC Global. On 13 November, the CFTC sued Louisiana and Arkansas over state actions against federally regulated event-contract venues; these are unresolved federal/state jurisdiction disputes, not final rulings or new crypto enforcement against platforms. FinCEN’s transaction statements remain agency findings, not court-adjudicated findings. Imperial Control 100 · Rebel Resistance 40, both held because the MEXC restriction is prospective and venue-specific and no broad change in self-custody/P2P capacity was verified.
